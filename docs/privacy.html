Privacy Policy

Kahani Ghar Publisher · last updated 29 October 2026

Who we are

Kahani Ghar Publisher is a private tool operated by the owner of the Kahani Ghar channel. Its only purpose is to publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What information the tool uses

  • TikTok: when the owner connects the channel's TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool the account's basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to publish videos to that account (user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload).
  • The same kind of publishing permission for the channel's own YouTube, Facebook and Instagram accounts.
  • The videos and captions the owner creates.

The tool does not collect, read or store any information about viewers, followers or any other person.

How it is stored

Access tokens are stored only in encrypted secrets of a private GitHub repository and in a private file on the owner's computer. They are used only to publish the channel's videos. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

How to remove access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) or in the settings of the other platforms. The tool keeps no copy of any data after access is revoked.
