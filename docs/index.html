Kahani Ghar कहानी घर

Kahani Ghar is a Hindi storytelling channel: short animated stories, a daily mystery series (“तेरहवाँ घंटा”) and stories from Indian mythology, posted as vertical videos on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Every video is made by the channel's owner with their own tools: the drawings, the animation and the sound are created by code, and the voices are synthetic (AI) voices, labelled as such on every platform.

Kahani Ghar Publisher

“Kahani Ghar Publisher” is a private tool used only by the channel's owner to publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts. It offers no service to other people and does not collect data from viewers.
